I am a [Senior AI Research Scientist](https://www.research.autodesk.com/people/yilin-liu/) at the [Autodesk AI Lab](https://www.research.autodesk.com/research-areas/science/ai-lab/), focusing on **3D generative AI** for design applications, including CAD understanding, generation, and editing. I am also a PhD student in the [SGP group](https://geometry.cs.ucl.ac.uk/index.php) at [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/), supervised by [Prof. Niloy Mitra](http://www0.cs.ucl.ac.uk/staff/n.mitra/index.html). My research explores general 3D shape understanding and **physics-driven** modelling using mLLMs. Before that, I was in the [GrUVi](https://gruvi.cs.sfu.ca) lab at [Simon Fraser University](https://www.sfu.ca), supervised by [Prof. Hao (Richard) Zhang](https://www.cs.sfu.ca/~haoz). I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science from the [VCC](https://vcc.tech/index.html) at [Shenzhen University](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://en.szu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Hui Huang](https://vcc.tech/~huihuang).
